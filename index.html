    Is the difference real?

    American Community Survey numbers are estimates with a margin of error (MOE). Use these tools before you say one place is “higher” than another or combine estimates.

    Estimate A
    Estimate B

    The coefficient of variation (CV) is the standard error as a share of the estimate. Common practice: under 15% is reliable, 15–30% use with caution, over 30% treat as unreliable. Thresholds vary by organization.

    Adding counts across counties, age groups, or categories? The combined MOE isn't the sum of the MOEs. Enter each estimate and its MOE.

    Uses the Census Bureau's approximation: combined MOE = √(MOE₁² + MOE₂² + …). It's an approximation, and less accurate when combining many estimates or estimates of zero.

    These tests assume the estimates are independent. Comparing a county to the state it's part of, or overlapping 5-year periods (2019–23 vs. 2020–24), needs a different approach. When in doubt, ask a data person.
